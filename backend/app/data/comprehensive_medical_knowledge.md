# Comprehensive Clinical Guidelines and Triage Knowledge Base

## Malaria (Infectious)
**Typical Symptoms:** High fever, chills, sweating, headache, nausea, vomiting.
**Recommended Specialty:** Internal Medicine
**Triage Guidelines & Actions:** Test for plasmodium. Prescribe ACTs. High urgency if severe.

## Typhoid Fever (Infectious)
**Typical Symptoms:** Prolonged high fever, fatigue, headache, nausea, abdominal pain, constipation or diarrhoea.
**Recommended Specialty:** Internal Medicine
**Triage Guidelines & Actions:** Widal test, blood culture. Prescribe antibiotics (Ciprofloxacin/Ceftriaxone).

## Cholera (Infectious)
**Typical Symptoms:** Severe watery diarrhoea, vomiting, leg cramps, rapid dehydration.
**Recommended Specialty:** Emergency Medicine
**Triage Guidelines & Actions:** Requires immediate IV fluids, ORS. Extremely high urgency.

## Hypertension (Cardiovascular)
**Typical Symptoms:** Often asymptomatic. Severe headaches, shortness of breath, nosebleeds in hypertensive crisis.
**Recommended Specialty:** Cardiologist
**Triage Guidelines & Actions:** Check BP. Routine monitoring. Emergency if BP > 180/120.

## Type 2 Diabetes (Endocrine)
**Typical Symptoms:** Increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, fatigue, blurred vision.
**Recommended Specialty:** Endocrinologist
**Triage Guidelines & Actions:** Fasting blood sugar. Metformin. Long term management.

## Asthma (Respiratory)
**Typical Symptoms:** Shortness of breath, chest tightness, wheezing, coughing attacks.
**Recommended Specialty:** Pulmonologist
**Triage Guidelines & Actions:** Inhalers (Salbutamol). High urgency during acute severe attack.

## Peptic Ulcer Disease (Gastrointestinal)
**Typical Symptoms:** Burning stomach pain, feeling of fullness, bloating, heartburn, nausea.
**Recommended Specialty:** Gastroenterologist
**Triage Guidelines & Actions:** PPIs, H. pylori eradication. Urgent if vomiting blood.

## Tuberculosis (Infectious)
**Typical Symptoms:** Cough lasting >3 weeks, chest pain, coughing up blood, fatigue, night sweats.
**Recommended Specialty:** Pulmonologist
**Triage Guidelines & Actions:** Chest X-ray, Sputum test. DOTS therapy. Isolate.

## Pneumonia (Respiratory)
**Typical Symptoms:** Cough with phlegm, fever, chills, difficulty breathing.
**Recommended Specialty:** Pulmonologist
**Triage Guidelines & Actions:** Antibiotics, oxygen therapy if severe.

## Appendicitis (Gastrointestinal)
**Typical Symptoms:** Sudden pain on right side of lower abdomen, nausea, vomiting, fever.
**Recommended Specialty:** General Surgeon
**Triage Guidelines & Actions:** Surgical emergency. Appendectomy required.

## Sickle Cell Crisis (Hematologic)
**Typical Symptoms:** Severe pain in chest, abdomen, joints, bones. Fatigue.
**Recommended Specialty:** Hematologist
**Triage Guidelines & Actions:** Analgesics, hydration, oxygen. Medical emergency.

## Eczema (Dermatologic)
**Typical Symptoms:** Dry, itchy, inflamed, and rough patches of skin.
**Recommended Specialty:** Dermatologist
**Triage Guidelines & Actions:** Topical corticosteroids, moisturizers. Low urgency.

## Glaucoma (Ophthalmologic)
**Typical Symptoms:** Loss of peripheral vision, halos around lights, eye redness, severe eye pain.
**Recommended Specialty:** Ophthalmologist
**Triage Guidelines & Actions:** Urgent if acute angle-closure. Eye drops to lower pressure.

## Myocardial Infarction (Cardiovascular)
**Typical Symptoms:** Pressure, tightness, pain in chest/arms spreading to neck/jaw, cold sweat.
**Recommended Specialty:** Cardiologist
**Triage Guidelines & Actions:** Absolute Emergency. Call ambulance. ECG, Troponin.

## Stroke (Neurological)
**Typical Symptoms:** Sudden numbness/weakness in face/arm/leg, confusion, trouble speaking.
**Recommended Specialty:** Neurologist
**Triage Guidelines & Actions:** Absolute Emergency. CT Scan. TPA if ischemic and within window.
